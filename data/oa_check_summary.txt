CORRECTIONS TO THE ASSUMPTIONS IN THE TASK BRIEF
------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. The Zotero export is at Litx/Exported Items/, not ICCAT/Exported Items/.

2. It holds 34 records but only ONE DOI, and that DOI is attached to the wrong
   PDF. The record 'Mourato et al. 2014 - Short-term movements ... sailfish'
   (DOI 10.1590/1982-0224-20130102, Neotropical Ichthyology) has as its attachment
   a byte-identical copy of ICCAT/Sailfish/Mourato et al 2023 Sailfish Brazil
   CV080080226.pdf - an ICCAT Collective Volume paper on Brazilian sailfish catch
   rates. The brief listed this file as a journal-article lookup target; it is not
   one. The DOI was not used, per the rule against guessing. The 2014 article is
   not present in this folder.

3. Three files the brief listed as journal articles are ICCAT Collective Volume
   papers, free from iccat.int, with no DOI:
     Garibaldi et al 2024  -> Collect. Vol. Sci. Pap. ICCAT 81(7), SCRS/2024/064
     Macias et al 2025     -> Collect. Vol. Sci. Pap. ICCAT 82(6), SCRS/2025/098
     Litx .../31108/Mourato -> Collect. Vol. Sci. Pap. ICCAT 80(8), SCRS/2023/092
   That reduces the lookup set from 16 to 13.

4. One journal article was not covered by any rule in the brief:
     Literature/CCSBT/Hoyle et al 2022 CPUE standardization for SBT in the Korean
     longline fishery.pdf - PeerJ, CC-BY 4.0, stated open access on page 1.
   It sits in CCSBT/ but is not an ESC paper. Added to the journal set.

5. The do-not-cite notice is not spread across ISC generally. It appears on 17 of
   51 documents, and every one is a Shark Working Group paper. ALBWG, BILLWG and
   PBFWG papers carry no such notice.

6. Traplines and meka-rings/CV08107064.pdf is byte-identical to the Garibaldi et
   al 2024 file in the same folder - a duplicate the brief did not list.

7. The Ochi et al. 2025 meka-ring trio: the Traplines/ copy and WGEB-03-RD-B are
   byte-identical; the IOTC copy is the same text with an IOTC-2025-WPEB21(AS)-26
   header. The IOTC copy is recorded as primary.

8. Literature/Traplines and meka-rings/Usui et al 2018 ... Japanese.pdf is the
   copyrighted Japanese original of the translation the brief excludes. It needs
   the same permission handling and is flagged uncertain, not shareable.

METHOD NOTES
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  - No file in Literature/ or Litx/ was modified, renamed, moved or deleted.
  - No full-text PDF was downloaded.
  - Duplicates were established by MD5 over file contents, not by filename.
  - Every PDF opened had a text layer; no OCR was needed and none was run.
  - No DOI was guessed or inferred from a filename.
